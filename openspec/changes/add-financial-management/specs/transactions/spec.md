# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Maintain a user's editable financial movements and expose searchable transaction history, including movements generated by savings pots and recurring payments.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Manage signed transactions
The system SHALL support creating, listing, retrieving, updating and deleting transactions with `name`, `categoryId`, `transactionDate` and signed `amount`. Names SHALL be nonblank and at most 100 characters. Amounts SHALL be nonzero, exact to at most two decimal places and within NUMERIC(19,2) range. Manual transactions SHALL require an owned active category.

#### Scenario: Record income and an expense
- **WHEN** a user creates transactions for `Salary` with amount `1200.00` and `Food` with amount `-45.50`
- **THEN** both records retain their supplied ISO dates, category IDs and signed amounts
- **AND** the system accepts either sign regardless of the user's current balance

#### Scenario: Reject invalid money
- **WHEN** an amount is zero, nonfinite, out of range or has more than two decimal places
- **THEN** the transaction is rejected without rounding or changing financial totals

### Requirement: Combine independent search and ordering
Transaction history SHALL support optional case-insensitive literal name search and one independent sort: `newest`, `oldest`, `name_asc`, `name_desc`, `amount_desc` or `amount_asc`. Date sorts SHALL use transaction date and amount sorts SHALL use signed numeric value. Equal sort values SHALL have deterministic ID ordering.

#### Scenario: Sort without search
- **WHEN** a user requests `amount_desc` without a search term
- **THEN** all effective transactions are ordered by signed amount descending with a stable ID tie-breaker

#### Scenario: Combine search and sort
- **WHEN** a user supplies a name fragment and `oldest`
- **THEN** only transactions matching the name fragment are returned, ordered by transaction date ascending
- **AND** `%` and `_` in the search term are treated as literal characters

### Requirement: Bound transaction history pages
History SHALL use zero-based pages with default `page=0`, `size=20` and `sort=newest`, with a maximum size of 100. Responses SHALL expose `items`, `page`, `size`, `totalElements` and `totalPages`. Invalid paging and sort values SHALL produce validation errors.

#### Scenario: Fetch the next filtered page
- **WHEN** a user requests page 1 of a filtered result with size 20
- **THEN** the response contains the next at most 20 matching records and metadata for the whole filtered result

### Requirement: Recalculate after transaction correction
All four business fields SHALL remain editable, including on automatically generated transactions. Updating or deleting a transaction SHALL update its effect on summaries and budgets atomically. Deletion SHALL exclude the transaction from effective history and calculations while preserving source linkage for consistency.

#### Scenario: Change category and sign
- **WHEN** a user changes a manual transaction's name, category, date and amount from `-40.00` to `25.00`
- **THEN** the updated fields are returned and the old expense is replaced by the new income
- **AND** budget spending is recalculated for the affected categories and months

#### Scenario: Move a transaction outside the current reporting cut
- **WHEN** a user changes an expense's date from inside the current summary period to outside it
- **THEN** it no longer contributes to that period's expenses or currentBalance
- **AND** it remains in transaction history and contributes to budgets according to its new calendar month

### Requirement: Create identifiable pot movements atomically
Each successful pot deposit SHALL create one positive transaction and each withdrawal one negative transaction in the same atomic operation. Pot movements SHALL expose `sourceType=POT` and `potId`, usable for translated frontend labels without requiring a synthetic personal category. Existing pot routes and response fields SHALL remain compatible.

#### Scenario: Deposit and withdraw
- **WHEN** a user deposits `100.00` and then withdraws `20.00` from an empty pot
- **THEN** the pot balance is `80.00` and exactly two transactions exist for that pot, with amounts `100.00` and `-20.00`
- **AND** failure to persist either side leaves both the pot and its transactions unchanged

### Requirement: Keep pot balance consistent with transaction corrections
Editing a pot movement's amount SHALL adjust its pot balance by the amount difference; deleting it SHALL reverse its contribution. Origin pot IDs SHALL remain immutable. Corrections SHALL preserve the existing nonnegative pot balance and maximum balance, returning a conflict if violated. Users SHALL be able to edit name, date and category without changing the origin.

#### Scenario: Correct a generated deposit
- **WHEN** a pot contains `100.00` from one deposit and its transaction is changed to `70.00`
- **THEN** both the pot balance and the transaction contribution become `70.00` atomically

#### Scenario: Correction would overdraw the pot
- **WHEN** reversing or editing a pot movement would make its balance negative
- **THEN** the operation returns a conflict and neither record changes

### Requirement: Preserve reversible pot deletion semantics
Deleting a pot SHALL exclude its linked transactions from effective history and financial calculations without creating an expense. Restoring it strictly before ten seconds SHALL reactivate its undeleted movements without duplicates. Expired restoration and access to hidden movements SHALL follow existing pot visibility rules.

#### Scenario: Delete and undo
- **WHEN** a pot with movements `100.00` and `-20.00` is deleted and restored within ten seconds
- **THEN** its contribution first disappears and then returns as `80.00`
- **AND** neither operation inserts an expense or a duplicate movement

### Requirement: Retain existing pot contributions during migration
Introducing transaction history SHALL preserve each existing pot's balance and represent a preexisting nonzero balance with one dated opening movement, identified as an opening snapshot rather than invented past activity. Its contribution to the financial summary SHALL follow the selected reporting cut like other movements. A deleted pot's opening movement SHALL remain excluded until a valid restoration.

#### Scenario: Upgrade a populated installation
- **WHEN** an existing active pot has balance `250.00` before the upgrade
- **THEN** it still has balance `250.00` afterwards and its opening transaction contributes exactly `250.00` in the period containing the migration date
- **AND** later cuts do not reset the stored pot balance or create further opening movements

### Requirement: Isolate transaction access and protect mutations
All transaction queries and mutations SHALL be scoped to the authenticated user and use the existing session and CSRF protections. Source metadata SHALL be assigned by the system; callers SHALL NOT forge another source, pot or payment association.

#### Scenario: Foreign or forged origin
- **WHEN** a user requests another user's transaction or attempts to assign an unauthorized source association
- **THEN** no foreign data is exposed and no financial record changes
